# PRD — Landing Experience v2
## Premium Hospitality Storytelling Experience

---

# Overview

The landing page is no longer simply a marketing homepage.

It becomes the **digital front door** of Cafein Today.

Every interaction should communicate the feeling of entering a premium coffee destination rather than browsing a conventional café website.

The experience should blend modern minimalism, luxury hospitality, cinematic storytelling, and interactive web design while maintaining excellent usability and performance.

The inspiration comes from premium hospitality brands such as AYANA Bali, Aman, Six Senses, Apple, and Linear—not by copying their design, but by adopting their philosophy of restraint, storytelling, typography, motion, and attention to detail.

---

# Design Philosophy

The design should emphasize:

- Simplicity
- Premium aesthetics
- Spacious layouts
- Elegant typography
- Cinematic interactions
- Smooth motion
- Storytelling through scrolling

The interface should feel calm, intentional, luxurious, and memorable.

Users should feel like they are exploring a premium destination, not simply navigating a website.

---

# Design Principles

## Minimalism

Reduce unnecessary visual elements.

Every component should have a purpose.

Whitespace becomes part of the design.

Avoid clutter.

---

## Motion Before Decoration

Motion should communicate hierarchy and context.

Animations should never exist solely for decoration.

Every movement should explain:

- where content comes from
- where it goes
- what changed

---

## Storytelling

Scrolling is part of the narrative.

Every section introduces a new chapter.

Transitions should feel continuous instead of disconnected.

---

## Luxury Hospitality

Every screen should feel curated.

Typography, spacing, lighting, photography, and motion should all reinforce a premium experience.

---

# Typography System

## Navigation

Replace the current condensed uppercase typography.

Navigation should feel understated.

Preferred font styles include:

- Geist
- Inter
- General Sans
- Satoshi
- Neue Montreal
- Suisse Intl

Characteristics:

- medium weight
- wider spacing
- excellent readability
- minimal styling

---

## Body Text

Current small typography feels overly technical.

Redesign all supporting text including:

- descriptions
- captions
- labels
- metadata
- helper text
- footer

Body text should:

- improve readability
- increase spacing
- reduce visual density
- create a softer luxury aesthetic

# Success Criteria

The landing page should:

- Immediately communicate a premium brand identity.
- Feel cinematic and immersive.
- Guide users through a narrative instead of a sequence of static sections.
- Encourage exploration through subtle interaction.
- Make the reservation flow feel exciting and memorable.
- Maintain excellent usability and accessibility.
- Preserve high performance despite advanced animations.
- Establish a cohesive visual language that can scale across the entire Cafein Today ecosystem.
